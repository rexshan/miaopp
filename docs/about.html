About PIPO Live

PIPO Live is a group voice chat app. It gives people an easy way to drop into a live voice room, talk with others in real time and stay connected with the people they meet.

What you can do on PIPO

  • Join live group voice rooms and talk with several people at the same time.
  • Meet new people and keep in touch with the ones you already know.
  • Support the hosts you like with virtual gifts inside the rooms.

Where to get PIPO

PIPO is available on iOS through the App Store, and on Android through the official APK provided on our home page. Always download the app from the official channels listed on this website.
