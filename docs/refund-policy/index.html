Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Refund Policy explains how consumer purchase and refund enquiries are handled for PIPO, operated by BOUNDLESS MOONLIGHT INTERNATIONAL LIMITED ("PIPO", "we", "us", or "our").

1. Scope and consumer rights

This policy covers purchases of PIPO Coins, virtual gifts, VIP benefits, and other digital items or paid services offered through PIPO or its official purchase channels. Separate creator or agency payout arrangements are governed by their own terms.

Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot be excluded under applicable consumer law. Any mandatory cancellation, withdrawal, refund, or other remedy available in your country or region continues to apply. App store rules and purchase-specific terms may also provide refund rights.

2. Digital delivery and change of mind

Coins are delivered when credited to the PIPO account specified in the order. Other digital items or services are delivered when made available or activated as described at purchase. Please check the recipient account, product, quantity, and price before paying.

Except where applicable law, app store rules, or the terms of your purchase provide otherwise, we generally do not offer change-of-mind refunds for digital items correctly delivered as ordered. Items already used, consumed, sent as gifts, or redeemed are generally not refundable on this basis. Delivery or activation alone does not remove any non-waivable statutory right.

3. When a refund may apply

We review requests involving the following circumstances:

  • Non-delivery: payment was completed, but the purchased items or service were not delivered and the issue cannot be remedied within a reasonable time or a period required by law.
  • Billing errors: a verified duplicate charge, incorrect amount, or other payment error. We will correct the error and refund any amount charged in excess of what was due.
  • Service problems: a material defect, incorrect delivery, service discontinuation, or other failure prevents use of the purchased service. Any refund or other remedy will reflect the affected purchase and applicable law.
  • Unauthorised or minor purchases: we will review the circumstances with the relevant payment provider, where necessary, under applicable law and payment rules.
  • Other rights: a refund, cancellation, or withdrawal is required by applicable law, app store rules, or the terms of the purchase.

4. Where to request a refund

Apple-billed purchases: use Apple's refund process. Apple handles these requests under its policies and applicable law. You may also contact PIPO for help identifying a purchase or resolving a delivery problem.

Google Play-billed purchases: use Google Play's refund request process or contact PIPO. Google Play and the developer may handle requests according to the purchase circumstances, Google Play rules, and applicable law.

Website and other official purchases: email service@aurozalive.com. We will review the order and coordinate with the relevant payment provider. If a separate authorised merchant is identified on your receipt, its refund process may also apply. Contact us if you need help identifying the merchant.

5. Information to provide

Send your request to service@aurozalive.com with the subject "PIPO Refund Request" and include:

  • Your PIPO user ID and the order or transaction number.
  • The purchase date, amount, currency, and payment channel.
  • The reason for your request and any relevant receipt or screenshot.
  • An email address where we can contact you.

We may ask for additional information reasonably necessary to verify the transaction and assess your request. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, full payment card numbers, or card security codes.

6. Review and return of funds

We will review your request as soon as reasonably practicable and tell you if further information is needed. We will notify you of the outcome and explain the reason if your request is declined. Any deadline required by applicable law will take priority.

Approved refunds will be returned through the original payment method wherever supported. If that method is unavailable, we will contact you about an available alternative consistent with applicable law and payment rules. The time for funds to appear depends on the app store, payment provider, and financial institution involved.

7. Effect of a refund and further enquiries

When a purchase is refunded, the corresponding Coins, items, or paid benefits may be removed or adjusted. Any adjustment will relate to the refunded purchase and comply with applicable law and payment rules.

Uninstalling PIPO or deleting an account does not automatically request or create a refund. You may still exercise any refund rights available to you. This policy does not restrict your right to contact your payment provider or use lawful complaint or payment dispute procedures.
